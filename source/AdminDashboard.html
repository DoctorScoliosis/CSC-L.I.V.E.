About L.I.V.E.

L.I.V.E., which stands for League Information & Viewing Engine, is a gaming information and viewing platform developed to help players, organizers, and viewers access important details about gaming events at Adventist University of the Philippines.

The system was published on July 26, 2026 and was initiated by Rex Dominic F. Perico, CSC President for S.Y. 2026-2027, as a project intended to support the university's sport community. L.I.V.E. provides a centralized space where users can check league information, game schedules, team details, match updates, standings, and other tournament-related announcements.

The platform was created to help make sports events more organized and easier to follow. Instead of depending on separate announcements and manual updates, users can refer to one system for relevant and updated information about ongoing and upcoming games.

Through L.I.V.E., the CSC hopes to contribute to the gradual improvement of sports and gaming-related activities in AUP by offering a more accessible and efficient way of sharing information. The system may also help strengthen communication between organizers and participants while providing students and viewers with a more convenient way to stay connected with gaming events.
